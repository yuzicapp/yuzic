Text size, spacing and corners now change the app as you pick them.

- Text size applies straight away instead of waiting for a restart, and icons grow with it
- Spacing reaches the lists that ignored it: the play queue, Quick picks, your Library rows, and the placeholders shown while a list loads
- The playing bar's artwork follows your corner style
- Albums with no artwork each get their own colour and initials, instead of one grey icon for all of them
