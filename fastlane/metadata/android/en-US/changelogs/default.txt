Fixes for tapping an artist you are listening to, and for headphone buttons.

- Tapping the artist on the player, for a track playing from an external source, opened a page that could not load. It now finds the artist where it actually lives
- The sheet for an external track gains Go to album and Go to artist, which only library tracks had
- A route change no longer pauses when one pair of headphones hands over to another — only when the sound would otherwise come out loud
